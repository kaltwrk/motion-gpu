Files are the original 1024 x 1024 JPEG maps, renamed only:
- wood-albedo.jpg: smoked_walnut_veneer_diff_1k.jpg (sRGB)
- wood-normal.jpg: smoked_walnut_veneer_nor_gl_1k.jpg (linear, OpenGL +Y)
- wood-roughness.jpg: smoked_walnut_veneer_rough_1k.jpg (linear)

This is a continuous walnut surface without board joints. The source is a
veneer scan; it supplies surface grain for the clock's solid wooden base.
A source tile covers 1 x 1 metre. Grain runs horizontally in the maps.
Any dark stain or finish adjustment is applied by the shader, not baked
into these source maps.

Direct download URLs and checksums are recorded in sources.json.

Original glass marking

stamp.svg and stamp.png are original artwork created for Spektral.
The fictional diamond S and 74 / 06 date marking do not reproduce a
historical manufacturer's trademark. The PNG is an antialiased linear
grayscale coverage mask: white is ink, black is unprinted glass. Sparse
static pinholes are authored in the SVG. No font or external artwork is used.
The original artwork is provided under the repository's MIT license.

Original cathodes

cathodes.svg and cathodes.png contain ten original curved wire drawings,
not glyphs taken from a font. The SVG records the paths; the PNG stores
linear unsigned distance to each path, for analytic wire and gas shading.
The original artwork is provided under the repository MIT license.
