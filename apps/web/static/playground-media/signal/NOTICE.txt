Space Center.

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30180

The source contains actual photographs taken aboard the International Space
Station on August 19, 2011, assembled by NASA into a time-lapse. Clouds and
city lights move through the view as the spacecraft travels above Earth.
This is camera imagery, not a computer-generated Earth animation.

NASA states that its astronaut photographs and time-lapse videos are not
protected by copyright unless otherwise noted and may be reproduced and
distributed with source acknowledgement. No third-party restriction appears
on this source page. This demo is not endorsed by NASA.

The local asset is an 18-second excerpt, seconds 5 through 23 of the source.
A native-resolution 4:3 crop was encoded as H.264 at 960 x 720, with no audio.
Source colors and time-lapse speed are unchanged. No generated frames,
interpolation, titles, logos, or overlays were added. See sources.json for
the original URL, exact crop, codec details, and SHA-256 checksums.
